Turbo Chef 2

Tap a dish to see where it's made, or tap a machine to see everything it makes.

Two identical ovens, four zones between them. Set three for the line and keep one open; either oven backs up the other, and together they run double at a rush.

Why one zone stays open

This zone is left free on purpose. It is your backup if an oven ever goes down, so the line keeps moving while the other one carries the load.

It is a second crisp zone when fries and wings are slamming, and it is room to run a special without changing anything else on the line.

A kitchen with a little slack is a kitchen that holds.

Internal planning tool. Prices and costs here are session-only and reset on reload. This is not the guest menu.
